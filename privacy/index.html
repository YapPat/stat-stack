Stat Stack

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

What stays on your device

Your XP, ranks, player collections, Daily streaks, scores, medals and settings (for every sport in the app) are stored only on your device. Stat Stack has no accounts and no servers of its own. We don't see, collect or sell this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Stat Stack is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob: banner ads on some screens, and optional video ads you choose to watch for an in-game boost. Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone)
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • information about the ads you see and tap, plus basic device and app information

Google uses this to show, measure and cap ads, to personalize ads where you allow it, and to prevent fraud. The advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking when iOS asks; you can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Players in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions that require it are asked for consent first, and can change their choice in the app under Settings → Privacy & credits → Ad privacy choices.

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your Daily Stack, Daily medal streak, Survival and Gold medal scores for each sport are sent to Apple under your Game Center nickname so they can appear on leaderboards. Apple's privacy policy covers this.

Notifications

Daily reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.

Children

Stat Stack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
