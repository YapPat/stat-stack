Stat Stack: Sports Trivia Quiz

Basketball, football, hockey and tennis. How much ball knowledge do you really have?

Every round, 10 real players spin in one at a time. You see their name and years, but not their stats. Lock each one into a multiplier slot before you know who's coming next, then chase Bronze, Silver, Gold or a Perfect board.

Four sports in one app

  • Basketball: points, assists, rebounds, steals, blocks, championships and MVPs
  • Football: passing, rushing and receiving yards and touchdowns, sacks, interceptions and MVPs
  • Hockey: goals, assists, points, playoff scoring, goalie wins and shutouts
  • Tennis: match wins, titles, majors, prize money and weeks at No. 1, for the men's and women's tours

Ways to play

  • Daily Stack: the same 10 players for everyone, one try a day, in every sport
  • Survival, a pick clock, team, decade and country Packs, and friend Challenges
  • 16,000+ real players to collect, a rank and themes for each sport
  • Game Center leaderboards

Unofficial fan game. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, tour, team or player.
