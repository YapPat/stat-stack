Basketball Stat Stack

How much ball knowledge do you really have?

Every round, 10 real players spin in one at a time. You see their name, position and years, but not their stats. Lock each one into a multiplier slot before you know who's coming next, then chase Bronze, Silver, Gold or a Perfect board.

  • Daily Stack: the same 10 players for everyone, one try a day
  • Survival, Shot Clock, team and decade Packs, and friend Challenges
  • Collect 2,800+ players, rank up from Rookie to GOAT and unlock themes
  • Game Center leaderboards and achievements
