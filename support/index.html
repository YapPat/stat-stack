How do I play?

Ten players spin in one at a time. Place each one into a multiplier slot (5× down to 1×) before you see their career total. Reach the goals for Bronze, Silver and Gold, or put everyone in the right slot for a Perfect board. Home → How to play has the full rules.

What's the Daily Stack?

Everyone gets the same 10 players each day, with one try. If you leave partway through, your picks are saved and you can finish it later that day.

Do I need an account or internet?

No account is needed. Rounds work offline. Ads and Game Center leaderboards need a connection.

Why don't I see the leaderboards?

Online leaderboards use Game Center. Sign in under Settings → Game Center on your iPhone, then open the Leaderboard tab again.

Where are the stats from?

Career regular-season totals for players with 100+ games, compiled from public historical records. If you spot a wrong number, email us.

How do I reset my progress?

Everything is saved only on your phone. You can clear the leaderboard in the app, and deleting the app erases everything else.
